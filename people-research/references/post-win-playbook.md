# Post-win playbook

Seeded Sept 24, 2026. A win collapses the broker funnel: the demo did Scout and Qualify for you. What remains is formalities (evidence capture) and conversion (parallel processes).

## Phase 1 — Formalities with the organizer (within 24–48h)
The win is only as useful as its documentation. Ask explicitly — organizers expect this.
- **Prize collection:** confirm amounts, delivery method, and timeline for gift cards / money. Get it in writing.
- **Social shoutout:** ask for a tweet/post from the organizer's account naming you and the team. Offer to draft it — reduces their effort to a copy-paste.
- **Certificate / written proof:** placement certificate or winner announcement suitable for future applications — the user collects these toward O1 visa evidence (extraordinary-ability documentation).
- **Evidence file:** save everything — announcement posts, screenshots, emails, photos of the stage/demo. Centralize it the same day; memory fades, links rot.

## Phase 2 — Convert the room (same day, then days after)
- **Chase judges and top-of-funnel people** while the win is fresh: the calendar-fill move — "can we put 30 minutes on the calendar next week?" Target: interviews, broker-target connections, recruiter calls.
- **Start interview processes in parallel.** The win is the warm intro to N companies at once; run them concurrently rather than sequentially. Parallel processes create leverage (competing timelines) that a single process never does.
- Prioritize: prize deciders and practitioner judges first (they saw the work up close), then organizers (they know everyone), then standout attendees.

## The winner's meal (e.g. judge invites you to Palo Alto)
Credibility is established and they made the first move — direct questions are correct. The meal starts at Activate.
- "What did you see in our demo that maps to problems your team actually has?"
- "We're talking to a few teams — what makes this one the right bet?"
- "What would I actually work on in the first 90 days?"
- "What's the team struggling with right now?"
- "What's the promotion scene like?"
- "Who would I work for — can I meet them before anything's decided?"
- "Here's the objection I get from larger companies — no scaled-product experience. How do you see it?"
- "What does the hiring process look like from here, and who decides?"
- Close: "What are the next steps, and on what timeline?"

## Victory reel (spec locked; editing method TBD)
- **Inputs:** raw vlog short videos from different times of the day (user has them).
- **Output:** one edited vertical reel, posted on the win — a cool story, not a montage.
- **Arc (day-arc from the clips):** morning arrival/energy → midday build tension → the demo screen → win announcement + reaction → one takeaway line / what's next.
- **Purpose:** credibility signaling to his network, and the asset DM'd with the good news to every mentor who helped him in SF.
- **Open items:** editing method (to be defined later); the mentor DM list — user deferred to later (compile from transcripts + memory when the time comes).

## Structural note
The four broker stages (Scout → Qualify → Activate → Sustain) still hold; the win lets you skip the first two for everyone who saw the demo. Sustain still applies afterward — report back outcomes to everyone who opened a door.
